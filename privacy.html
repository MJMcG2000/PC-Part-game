Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Rigdle is a free browser game. This page explains, in plain English, what data is involved when you play and who handles it.

No accounts, no Rigdle servers

Rigdle has no sign-up, no login and no backend database. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details, and the game itself never sends your guesses or results to us.

What's stored on your device

Your progress is saved in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. That includes today's guesses, your Endless puzzle, your win/streak statistics, your settings (colorblind mode, hard mode, which mode you last played, whether you've seen the help screen) and a cache of game cover-image links. This data never leaves your browser unless you choose to share it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, and it isn't synced between devices.

When you tap Share result, your result is copied to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Game images

To show game artwork, your browser loads images directly from Steam's image servers (Valve) and, for some console games, looks up cover art on Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). Like any website you load content from, those services receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Rigdle shows ads through Google AdSense to cover its costs. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure them and limit how often you see the same ad. Depending on your location and choices, the ads may be personalized based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Opt out of personalized ads from Google: adssettings.google.com
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time.

Hosting

Rigdle is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare may collect basic technical information (such as IP addresses, request logs and aggregate traffic statistics) to deliver the site, keep it secure and measure traffic. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Children

Rigdle is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
