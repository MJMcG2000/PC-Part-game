Part tiers

How Rigdle ranks parts, and what the tile colors and arrows are measuring.

The short version

  • Each brand has its own ladder: every CPU or graphics card of that brand, ranked from weakest to strongest by gaming performance. A part's tier is its rung on that ladder.
  • Tiles compare your part with the right answer on the same brand's ladder. Green is the answer. Yellow is one rung away. Gray is two or more rungs away.
  • ⬆ means your part isn't fast enough, so go up the ladder. ⬇ means it's faster than needed, so go down.
  • The ladder follows performance, not release date. The pickers list parts newest first, so the next rung is often not the next part in the list. Switch Sort parts to By tier above the pickers to list them in ladder order, strongest first.

Why release date is misleading

A newer part isn't always a faster one. Budget cards and chips launched years after a flagship are often slower than it. These pairs are next to, or near, each other on their ladders but out of order by date:

    Graphics cards and video memory (VRAM)

    Games need a certain amount of video memory, and more at higher resolutions (×1.2 at 1440p, ×1.5 at 4K). A card with less than that loses 40% of its frame rate. So a card high on the ladder can still fall short. For example, an 8 GB card in a game that needs 9.6 GB can lose to an older 12 GB card. When that happens, the GPU slot shows an orange VRAM tag. If the card is above the answer on the ladder but still misses the target, its tile is gray with ⬆: you need a card with more memory, not a weaker one. Each card's memory is listed next to its name in the picker.

    RAM

    RAM has a single ladder: . The answer is the smallest amount that meets the game's recommended RAM. Its type (SDR through DDR5) just follows the CPU and doesn't affect tiers.

    Either brand can win

    AMD and Intel CPUs are on separate ladders, and so are NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards. Each brand has its own minimum part, and either one counts as correct. If no part of the brand you picked is fast enough, the tile is gray with ⬆: switch brands.

    The tier lists

    Scores are relative gaming performance: a Ryzen 5 1600 is 100 for CPUs and an RTX 4060 is 100 for GPUs. Tier 1 is the weakest. Step is how much faster a part is than the tier below it.
